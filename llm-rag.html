FAQ

LLM & RAG, answered

Will our data train a public model?

We design for your data to stay private: your cloud or VPC, and provider settings or models that do not train on your data. Specifics depend on your requirements and are agreed up front.

Can it respect existing permissions?

Yes. Retrieval can be filtered by user and group so people only get answers from content they are allowed to see.

How do you stop it making things up?

Grounding in retrieved sources, citations, refusal when evidence is thin, and evaluation tests. No system is perfect, so we measure and monitor.

Which models do you use?

We are model-agnostic and choose per use case, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and open-source models.
