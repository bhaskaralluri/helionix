FAQ

Agents, answered

What is the difference between an agent and a chatbot?

A chatbot answers. An agent plans multi-step work, uses tools such as your CRM or ticketing system, and takes actions, under rules and approvals you define.

Will agents replace my team?

Our designs keep people in control: agents handle repetitive work and escalate edge cases and sensitive decisions to humans.

Where does our data go?

You choose: your cloud, a private VPC or on-prem where required. We apply least-privilege access and can work under NDA.

How quickly can we see a first agent?

A scoped pilot typically reaches a working first workflow in weeks, depending on integrations and data access.
