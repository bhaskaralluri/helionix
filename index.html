FAQ

Questions, answered

What is GEO, and how is it different from SEO?

SEO earns rankings in traditional search results. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) earns mentions and citations inside AI-generated answers from tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. They share foundations, so we do both together.

Can you guarantee we'll be recommended by ChatGPT?

No one honestly can: these systems are probabilistic and change often. What we do is systematically improve the signals that influence them, then measure your share of answers over time and report it transparently.

How long does it take to see results?

Technical and content improvements can show movement in weeks; authority-driven gains build over a few months. Agent projects typically ship a first production workflow within weeks, then expand.

Is our data safe when you build AI agents?

Security is designed in from day one: least-privilege access, encryption, audit logging and deployment options including your own cloud or on-prem. We can work under NDA and your compliance requirements.

Which AI models do you work with?

We are model-agnostic and choose per use case, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and open-source models, so you avoid vendor lock-in.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Most clients continue with an optimization and operations retainer covering monitoring, evaluation, new use cases and reporting.
