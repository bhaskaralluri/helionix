FAQ

GEO & SEO, answered

Do you guarantee we will be recommended by AI?

No one honestly can. These systems are probabilistic and change often. We improve the signals that influence them and report your share of answers transparently over time.

Do I need GEO if I already do SEO?

Strong SEO is a foundation, but AI answers weigh additional signals such as entity clarity, citable evidence and third-party mentions. GEO builds on your SEO rather than replacing it.

Which AI platforms do you cover?

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Overviews and others relevant to your audience.

How is progress reported?

A monthly report covering share-of-answer, citation rate, sentiment, rankings, traffic and the pipeline tied to them, with clear next actions.
