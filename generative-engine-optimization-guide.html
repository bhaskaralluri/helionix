AI GEO & SEO

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): a practical guide

How AI assistants decide who to recommend, how GEO differs from SEO, and a practical checklist for earning citations in AI answers.

Helionix AI Systems · November 2026 · 10 min read

When someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews for "the best provider of X", they often get a short answer with a handful of named brands, and many never click further. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of improving the odds that your brand is among the ones named and cited.

GEO vs SEO

Traditional SEOGEO
GoalRank a page in a list of linksBe mentioned and cited in a generated answer
Unit of successPosition and click-throughShare of answer, citation rate, sentiment
Key signalsKeywords, backlinks, technical healthEntity clarity, evidence, authority, structure, freshness
Content shapePages optimised for a queryPassages a model can extract and quote

GEO builds on SEO rather than replacing it: AI systems that browse or retrieve rely heavily on web content that is crawlable, fast and well structured.

How AI answers pick sources (in general terms)

Different engines work differently and change often, but most combine what the model learned in training with retrieval of fresh pages. Sources that tend to be favoured are clear, specific, well-corroborated and easy to extract from. Treat any claim of a guaranteed formula with suspicion, including from vendors.

A practical GEO checklist

1. Make your entity unmistakable

  • Consistent name, description and facts across your site and profiles.
  • Organization, Product, Service and FAQ schema markup where relevant.
  • An About page that states plainly who you are and what you do.

2. Write answer-ready content

  • Lead with the direct answer, then explain.
  • Use clear headings that match real questions buyers ask.
  • Include original data, definitions, comparisons and examples that are worth citing.
  • Keep key pages fresh and dated.

3. Be easy to crawl

  • Fast, accessible, server-rendered pages with clean internal links.
  • Do not block legitimate AI crawlers in robots.txt if you want to be cited.
  • XML sitemap and sensible canonical URLs.

4. Earn third-party corroboration

  • Reviews, directories, industry publications and expert mentions that confirm what you say.
  • Consistent presence where your buyers and the models look.

5. Measure it

  • Maintain a list of buyer questions and test them regularly across engines.
  • Track whether you are mentioned, cited, and how you are described.
  • Tie visibility to traffic and pipeline, and iterate.
A note on honesty

No one can guarantee a recommendation from an AI system. Good GEO improves the signals you control and measures the outcome, and a trustworthy partner will say so.
